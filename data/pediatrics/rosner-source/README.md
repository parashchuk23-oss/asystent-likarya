# Bernard Rosner pediatric blood pressure model

Source: https://sites.google.com/a/channing.harvard.edu/bernardrosner/pediatric-blood-press/childhood-blood-pressure

Retrieved: 2026-09-14

These are unmodified author-provided source files used to validate the JavaScript port:

- `childhoodbppct.sas`
- `quantreg_coef.txt`
- `ht_zscore.txt`
- `ht_zscore_inf.txt`

Clinical reference: AAP 2017 Clinical Practice Guideline for Screening and Management of High Blood Pressure in Children and Adolescents.

Do not edit the clinical coefficients manually. The SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `rosnerBloodPressureModelData.js`.
